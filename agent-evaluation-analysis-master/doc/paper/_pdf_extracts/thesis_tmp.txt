摘要 
I 
摘要 
随着军事科技的发展和现代战争的复杂化， 人工智能技术的引入对军事决策具有重
要意义。 然而， 深度神经网络的 “黑盒” 特性， 使得模型可解释性成为广泛研究的议题。
因此本文以规则演化理论为基础， 结合军事博弈现实需求， 以知识和数据相结合的训练
方法进行多智能体策略学习， 并应用于典型复杂博弈对抗场景中。 论文的主要研究工作
如下： 
一、 针对多智能体博弈对抗场景中智能体决策模型的解释性问题， 设计了一种基于
学习分类器系统的智能体决策模型。 该模型由分类器子系统和规则发现子系统组成， 其
中分类器子系统采用规则的方式实现智能体决策， 规则发现子系统提高规则库的多样性；
同时设计了规则评估机制指导模型学习。 在空战博弈场景下， 该模型提高了智能体对抗
性能，模型本身具有较强的解释性。 
二、 针对深度强化学习模型生成的策略解释性差的问题， 提出一种基于规则的策略
提取与解释深度强化学习模型策略的方法。 使用强化学习模型进行博弈对抗生成对抗数
据， 基于对抗数据采用策略提取方法将强化学习模型策略提取成一个决策树策略， 通过
决策树解释方法进行规则提取进而实现模型的可解释性。 在典型博弈对抗场景下， 提取
出的决策树模型在对抗效能上 能完全替代强化学习模型，并在对抗 胜率上有一定提高。
此外，针对两种对抗环境，提取决策树中的策略规则，构建策略规则库，建立博弈对抗
策略决策树。 
三、为了获得更多的博弈对抗策略，将提取出的决策树模型作为专家决策树，设计
一种基于遗传算法的决策树演化方法， 利用遗传算法进行解空间的探索， 以增强决策的
多样性，实现知识与数据协同驱动。在典型博弈对抗场景下，经演化后的决策树在对抗
效能方面都有不同程度的提升， 通过对决策树的规则提取与分析， 获得新增的策略规则
表，进一步充实博弈对抗策略。 
关键词：多智能体，规则学习，决策树，遗传算法，博弈对抗

Abstract 
III 
Abstract 
With the development of military technology and the increasing complexity of modern 
warfare, the introduction of artificial intelligence  (AI) technology is of great significance to 
military decision-making. However, the "black box" characteristic of deep neural networks has 
made model interpretability an important issue. Therefore, the strategy learning problem in the 
multi-intelligence game confrontation scenario is investigated in this paper. Based on the rule  
evolution theory, combined with the real needs of military gaming, this paper uses a training 
method that combines knowledge and data and applies it to complex game confrontation 
scenarios to verify its effectiveness.  
The main research contents of the thesis are as follows: 
Firstly, a decision -making model for intelligent agents based on a learning classifier 
system is designed  to a ddress the interpretability issue  of decision models in multi -agent 
adversarial scenarios. This model consists of two subsystems: a classifier subsystem and a rule 
discovery subsystem. The classifier subsystem employs rules to determine the decisions of the 
intelligent agents, while the rule discovery subsystem enhances the diversity of the rule set . 
Additionally, a rule evaluation mechanism has been designed to guide model learning. In the 
context of aerial combat gaming scenarios, this model has demonstrated improvements in the 
combat performance of intelligent agents while also possessing strong interpretability. 
Secondly, a method based on rule-based policy extraction and interpretation for deep 
reinforcement learning  (DRL) model policies is proposed to address the problem of poor 
interpretability of DRL strategies. By utilizing reinforcement learning model policies in 
adversarial gaming scenarios to generate adversarial data, a policy extraction method is 
employed to extract the reinforcement learning model policy into a decision tree policy . This 
decision tree policy is then interpreted using decision tree interpretation methods to extract rules, 
thereby achieving model interpretability. In the context of dual-ship anti-air warfare scenarios, 
the extracted decision tree model completely replaces the DRL model in terms of operational 
efficacy, with a notable improvement in the win rate during battles. Additionally, tactical rules 
are extracted from the decision tree to construct a tactical rule repository, facilitating the 
establishment of adversarial gaming strategies in two combat environments. 
Thirdly, in order to obtain a broader range of adversarial gaming strategies, the extracted 
decision tree model is utilized as an expert decision tree, and a decision tree evolution method 
based on genetic algorithm is designed to realize the collaborative driving of knowledge and  
data. This approach utilizes genetic algorithms to explore solution spaces, thereby enhancing 
the diversity of decision -making. In the context of dual -ship anti -air warfare scenarios, the 
evolved decision trees exhibit varying degrees of improvement in operational efficacy. By 
extracting and analyzing rules from the decision trees, a new set of tactical rule s is obtained, 
further enriching the repertoire of adversarial gaming strategies. 
Keywords: multi-agent, rule learning, decision tree, genetic algorithm, game confrontatio
